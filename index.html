What we do

  • Endpoint detection and responseAdvisory, tuning, API automation and threat hunting for SentinelOne deployments.
  • Vulnerability prioritizationRisk-based reporting that ranks findings by real-world exploitability, including EPSS, so teams patch what attackers are most likely to use.
  • Security tooling and trainingCustom integrations and reporting tools built on security platform APIs, and training for the teams who run them.

How we work

All of our work is defensive. We work only on systems our clients own or are authorized to assess, under written engagement terms, and we handle client data under confidentiality agreements.

We deliver services directly and through partner firms that contract Progability for specialist engagements.
